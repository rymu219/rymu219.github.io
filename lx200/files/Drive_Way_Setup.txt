THE DRIVE WAY: SET UP A BOT FROM A JOB DESCRIPTION FILE
Take-home steps from "Your Project Already Told You It Would Slip" (Ryan Murphy)

This is how Ryan runs his own Bots. Instead of pasting a prompt, each Bot's job description is a file in Google
Drive. Setup is one line, and changing how a Bot works means editing the file.

About 20 minutes. Everything in the practice pack is fictional.


BEFORE YOU START
- Use a personal or test Google account. A company account may block the connection or need IT approval.
- Read the permission screen. To read files, Grok's Drive connection asks for "see, edit, create and delete all
  your Google Drive files." Decide whether you're comfortable with that for this account. If not, use a fresh
  test account, or stay with the paste-it-in way.
- Move anything private out of that Drive first, or accept that your Bots can read it. Your instructions tell a
  Bot where to look; they don't lock anything.


1. MAKE YOUR OWN COPY OF THE WORLD
   a. In your Google Drive, create a folder named:  LX-200 World
   b. Open the practice folder. For each of these, right-click > Make a copy, then move the copy into your folder
      and remove "Copy of" from the name:
      - LX-200_World (the sheet)
      - Hengtai_Quotation_LX-200.pdf
      - LX-200_Drawing_RevB_SIGNED.pdf
      - LX-200_Drawing_RevC.pdf
      - Scout instructions.txt, Ledger instructions.txt, Foreman instructions.txt
   c. In your copy of LX-200_World, open the Findings tab and delete every row below the header.
      If the Inbox tab has a message 67, delete that row too.
   d. In each instructions file, replace "Ryan" with your name, and LX Scout / LX Ledger / LX Foreman with the
      names you'll give your Bots. Each file checks that it's loaded into the right Bot, and Foreman finds its
      teammates by name.

2. CONNECT GOOGLE IN GROK BOT
   a. Open Connect apps. Add Google Sheets, click Authorize, sign in, and approve.
   b. Do the same for Google Drive. Sheets and Drive connect separately.
   c. If it also asks for Calendar or anything else you don't need, say no. Grant only what the Bot needs.
   d. Check that both show as Installed.

3. CREATE THE BOTS (ONE LINE EACH)
   Create a new Bot and send, as its first message:

   You're Scout. Your job description is the file "Scout instructions.txt" in the Google Drive folder
   "LX-200 World". Read it, follow it from now on, and remember it. Then tell me in three lines what your job is
   and which files you're allowed to open.

   Do the same for Ledger and Foreman, changing the name and the file.
   A good answer lists the LX-200_World sheet, the three PDFs and its own instructions file, and says the only
   change it may make is adding rows to the Findings tab.

4. RUN THE TEAM (ONE LINE, TO FOREMAN)
   Foreman: "Run the team."
   Foreman messages Scout and Ledger, waits until both have posted to Findings, then writes the brief.
   If a teammate never answers, send that Bot "Run your review" yourself, then tell Foreman "Both have posted."
   Compare what they find with the checkpoint slides.

5. ADD MESSAGE 67 YOURSELF
   a. Open Build_Along_Bot_Prompts.txt in the practice folder and find MESSAGE 67.
   b. In your LX-200_World sheet, add it as a new row at the bottom of the Inbox tab
      (Message 67, Nov 23, 2026, From, To, Subject, and the body).
   c. Foreman: "A new message came in."  It tells Scout and Ledger to check, waits for their NEW rows, and updates
      the brief.
   Watch for the rows that start with "NEW: ".

6. CHANGE HOW A BOT WORKS
   Edit its instructions file in Drive, then tell the Bot: "Reload your instructions."

7. WHEN YOU'RE DONE
   Remove Grok's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or keep it if you'll keep
   using it. Approvals in Grok Bot are the control; instructions are not.


WHY THE LANES
Each file says what the Bot's job is, what is NOT its job, and what to do when an input is missing. Without that,
a Bot that's missing an input quietly does another Bot's work, and you can't tell which finding came from where.
And loading a file replaces a Bot's job, so each file checks it's in the right Bot first.

USING IT ON YOUR OWN WORK
- Swap the sheet for your own: an Inbox tab (or a connected inbox), your board tabs, and a Findings tab.
- Keep the file list in each instructions file exact. "Open only these files" beats "anything in the folder."
- Keep the one-change rule: Bots add rows to Findings and change nothing else. You decide what happens next.
